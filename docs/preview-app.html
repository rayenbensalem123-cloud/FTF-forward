How to click

  • Flip Staff / Player at the top — tabs, home content, reports and profile all change.
  • Staff home is the banner plus four cards. Player home is the banner plus her form card.
  • Use the bottom tabs to move around. On Dashboard, the four cards open Calendar, Reports, the Bingo board and News.
  • On Profile, Team and Work rows open Squad, Staff, Camps, Meetings and Career (staff) or the nine-row Tools card (player). ‹ goes back.
  • On Squad, the pills and chips filter the list; tapping a player opens her sheet.
  • On Reports, tapping a verify pill flips it (staff). The timer ticks live. Switches toggle.
  • Nothing here is wired to data — approve the structure and I build it for real.
