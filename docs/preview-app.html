FTF Forward — clickable prototype

Two phones: staff on the left, player on the right. Everything is tappable: the tabs, the home cards, the Squad filters and player rows (a sheet opens), the tool rows, the report verify pills, the switches, the games. Both timers tick live. Data is mock but shaped exactly like the app's.

How to click

  • Left phone is staff (5 tabs), right phone is player (4 tabs).
  • On Dashboard, the four cards open Calendar, Reports, the Bingo board and News. Player home is the banner plus her form.
  • On Profile, Team and Work rows open Squad, Staff, Camps, Meetings, Career (staff) or the nine-row Tools card (player). ‹ goes back.
  • On Squad, the pills and chips filter; tapping a player opens her sheet. Games lock, reveal and guess.
  • On Reports, tapping a verify pill flips it (staff). Both timers tick. Switches toggle.
  • Nothing here is wired to data — approve the structure and I build it for real.
