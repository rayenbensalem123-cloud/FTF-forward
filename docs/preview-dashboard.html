What changed

  • One button, three variants. A shared Button component: primary (gold fill, navy text), secondary (gold outline, gold text) and ghost. Same height, radius, tracking and weight everywhere -- the hero, the feature cards and anywhere else now use it instead of each rolling its own.
  • The hero has the match-day weather. A WeatherChip loads the Open-Meteo forecast for the venue via lib/weather and shows the high/low with a condition icon. It geocodes the venue name, caches for three hours, and renders nothing when the match is beyond the forecast range or the lookup fails -- never a wrong number.
  • Logos are shown directly. The header crest and the hero crests no longer sit on white or blue discs. A faint translucent backing lifts them off the gradient without reading as a coloured badge behind the logo.
